Why this package

The first two weeks at home are the hardest.

Your body is healing. Your baby needs care. You need rest. After the hospital, many mothers are left to manage all of this alone.

Izimom sends a licensed nurse or midwife to your home every day for 15 days. She helps you recover, care for baby, feed with confidence, and feel supported — physically and emotionally.

The same person visits you each day, so you are not starting over with a stranger.

What our 15-day care includes

Specially crafted for you

Everything you need in the first two weeks, explained simply.

Physical recovery support

We help your body heal after birth, including after a C-section, and tell you when to go back to the clinic.

Breastfeeding assistance

We help you feed baby with less pain and less worry.

Baby bathing, diaper change & cord care

We bathe baby, change diapers, and care for the cord — and show you how to do it too.
